Thank you for installing Caecilia.

WHAT YOU ARE INSTALLING
  •  Caecilia VST3  — load it in any VST3 host (Cubase, Reaper, Studio One,
     Bitwig, Ableton via VST3, …). Installed into the shared 64-bit VST3 folder.
  •  Caecilia Standalone  — play the organ without a DAW, straight from the
     Start menu.

HIGHLIGHTS
  •  A three-division romantic organ with per-tonal-family voicing.
  •  A living console: draw a stop and the sound changes; the keys light as
     you play; the wind breathes under polyphony.
  •  Algorithmic hall reverb and equal-tempered tuning.
  •  Built-in demo pieces (J. S. Bach) that play with the keys lighting up.

AFTER INSTALLING
  •  Rescan your plug-ins in your DAW so Caecilia (VST3, Instrument) appears.
  •  The Standalone needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which ships with
     Windows 10/11. If the console looks blank, install the free "Evergreen
     WebView2 Runtime" from Microsoft and relaunch.
